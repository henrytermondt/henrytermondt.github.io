About Me

About

As a part of Rice University’s class of 2030, I’m studying CS and planning on minoring in Computational and Applied Mathematics as well as Physics. I’m especially interested in parallel computing and its application in science.

I build simulations and visualizations in WebGL/GLSL and JavaScript, and I’m transitioning to Rust, C++, and CUDA. I also have experience with Python and Java.

For fun, I enjoy playing violin and folding origami. I also find tutoring and helping others extremely rewarding.
